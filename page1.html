ABOUT SDG 16

Peaceful Societies. Fair Justice.

Sustainable Development Goal 16 promotes peaceful and inclusive societies, access to justice, and effective institutions.

🕊️ Why Peace Matters

Peace creates communities where people can live, learn, work, and grow without fear or violence.

✓ Reduce violence
✓ Protect communities
✓ Promote inclusion

⚖️ Why Justice Matters

Justice gives people equal opportunities to receive fair treatment and legal protection.

✓ Equal access to justice
✓ Protect human rights
✓ Fight discrimination
STRONG INSTITUTIONS

Trust Through Transparency

Strong institutions are transparent, accountable, inclusive, and responsive to citizens.

🔍

Transparency

Make information accessible so citizens can understand how decisions are made.

🔐

Accountability

Institutions should be responsible for their decisions and actions.

🤝

Inclusion

Give people opportunities to participate in decisions that affect their communities.
